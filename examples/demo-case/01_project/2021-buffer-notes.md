# Project PRJ-2021 Buffer Study

Author: Li Wei; Zhang Min
Year: 2021

## Materials

Tris base, NaCl, EDTA, and ultrapure water were used for all preparations.

## Buffer recipe

The final buffer recipe was 50 mM Tris-HCl, 150 mM NaCl, 1 mM EDTA at pH 7.4.
Prepare 1 L at room temperature and filter through a 0.22 um membrane.

## Notes

The 2021 batch was stored at 4 C for two weeks and remained clear.
